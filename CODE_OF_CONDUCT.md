# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the
work, not about the person doing it.

## What we expect

Technical disagreement is the point of a review, and it should be direct.
"This will misattribute a virtual call" is a useful sentence. "You clearly
did not think about this" is not, and it is not more direct, only less
useful.

Concretely:

- Critique the change, the design or the claim. Not the author.
- When you say something is wrong, say why, and say what would be right.
- Assume good faith on the first reading. Ask before concluding.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. A no with a reason is a
  complete answer.
- Credit the people whose work you build on, in commits and in prose.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, demeaning comments, sustained disruption of a
discussion, unwelcome attention, publishing private information, and
conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional
setting.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments,
chat, and any space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Email `yzhao102@stevens.edu` with `PARAPET conduct` in the subject.
Reports go to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md);
a report about a maintainer goes to the others, and that maintainer takes
no part in handling it.

While there is one maintainer, that last sentence has nothing behind it.
It is written down anyway, because the rule has to exist before the case
does. Adding a second maintainer is what makes it real.

Tell us what happened, where, and when. Include links if there are any.

We will acknowledge within three working days and tell you what we decide.
We will keep your identity to the people handling the report unless you
agree otherwise or the law requires more.

We will not act on anonymous reports as a rule, because we usually cannot
check them, but we will read them and they may inform what we watch for.

## What happens next

Depending on what we find: a private conversation, a public correction, a
temporary block, or removal from the project. We will say which and why to
the person concerned, and we will say something to the reporter.

An appeal goes to the maintainers who did not decide the original case.

## Scope of this document

This is a small project and this is a short policy. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1, cut
down to what we can actually enforce, because a policy nobody can act on
is worse than a short one that they can.
